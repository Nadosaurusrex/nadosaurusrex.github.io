Talent is everywhere. What's rare is a way in.

For a long time, to learn or build anything you first had to fit: the money, the papers, the language, the speed. AI is the first thing you can put in someone's hands that doesn't ask them to fit first. It still only arrives if a person hands it over and stays a while. That's what I mean by access, and this blog is me working out, in public, how to get it to the people who are always left out.

Me, briefly: Milan, then Dublin, Paris, and now London. Trained as an aerospace engineer; these days I code, build things and wander into fields that aren't mine. I work at one of the AI labs; what's written here is my own. Also, for no good reason, I have an obsession with submarines and the Battle of the Atlantic so you might read some of that.

If you want me to come and show it at a school, a library or to your team, write to me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nadosaurusrex).
