[In January I wrote]({{ site.baseurl }}/p/laccesso-non-il-talento/) that if you can read, you can get ahead. I thought AI was there to serve humanity and could help the weak. After months of study, I think the frontier is heading toward research. And yes, a cure for cancer helps them too, but the people who are behind stay behind, because nobody rebuilds the place where they live.

[![METR chart: the length of software tasks that models can complete 50% of the time, from 2019 to 2026. The line rises exponentially.]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/di-villaggio-in-villaggio/metr-time-horizons.png)](https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/)

Why do I think this? Look at this chart. [It's made by METR](https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/), and it measures how long a task a model can finish on its own. More compute, more data, more intelligence: from GPT-4 onward the line explodes, and in 2026 it doubles every few months. That intelligence goes to the labs, to research, to bio. Meanwhile the guy who has to translate a form from the town hall already does it with a free model. That problem is solved. But his life doesn't move up a level: he translates the form, and that's it. Someone who works with the frontier has a tool that does research with them, and every few months that gap doubles, like the line on the chart. He doesn't fall behind because he lacks the tool. He falls behind because his is standing still and everyone else's is running.

So what is he missing? Not intelligence. That's there, and it's free. He's missing someone who rebuilds the place where he lives, not [someone who explains the chat to him]({{ site.baseurl }}/p/lintelligenza-che-sta-con-loro/). In the labs it already happens: there are engineers who walk in, watch how people work and build the system around them. At the town hall, in the factory, in the slow places, there's nobody. We've left the era when software was sold as a subscription. We're in the one where what matters is who has understood what's coming, something as big as electricity, and goes from village to village redesigning how things work. The gap isn't in the model. It's between the model and these people's lives, and someone has to go there. I want to be one of them.
