ONE include with three outputs, so the twin lookup lives in one place and nothing depends on variables leaking between templates: [include lang.html part="head" ] in : hreflang alternates (+ x-default) and the soft auto-route script [include lang.html part="switch" ] in the header nav: the EN · IT switch [include lang.html part="origin" ] in the post header: the "written in … · other edition" line Pairing rule. Two POSTS are twins when they share `ref` (if set; falls back to `slug` when the ref finds nothing) or else `slug`, and differ in `lang`. The origin line's statement can be overridden per post with front matter `edition_note:` (piece 1 uses it on the English edition). Two other PAGES are twins when they share `ref`. `lang` defaults to site.lang (en) via _config.yml defaults.
